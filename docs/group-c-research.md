# Group Design C — customer experience research worksheet

The project overview requires **three short sessions with real users** on an existing online course site. Do not submit this worksheet as completed research until participants have been observed and their responses recorded.

## Session setup

Choose one existing course platform and use the same site and tasks for all three participants. Record the platform URL, device, date, and participant code (P1–P3). Avoid collecting names or private account information.

Suggested tasks:

1. Find a course that interests you and locate its price and syllabus.
2. Find where you would review a purchase or receipt.
3. Find how to continue a course and check your progress.
4. Find an answer to a support question and describe how you would contact support.

Record whether each task was completed, where the participant hesitated, and any short verbatim comments. If the existing site requires payment, stop before purchase.

## Shortened UEQ

After the tasks, ask each participant to rate the site on each seven point pair. **1** means the left word fits completely; **7** means the right word fits completely.

The first four pairs cover pragmatic quality; the last four cover hedonic quality. With only three participants, treat the averages as directional observations rather than representative statistics. Note the highest and lowest scoring items and relate each finding to an observed task.
